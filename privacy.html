Privacy Policy.

Effective September 5, 2026

One Rep Closer LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected through this website, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you submit the website inquiry form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, service interest, and preferred contact method. Please do not submit medical records, diagnoses, medication information, or other sensitive health information through the website form.

The website and its service providers may also process limited technical information, such as device type, browser information, IP address, and basic usage or security data required to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, provide requested service information, arrange consultations or training, maintain appropriate business records, protect the website, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

How information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, security, and embedded content. These include WebsitePublisher.ai and, when you view the Instagram section, Elfsight and Instagram/Meta. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or respond to lawful requests.

Embedded content and external links

The website includes an embedded Instagram feed and may link to third-party websites. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. One Rep Closer LLC does not control third-party privacy practices.

Data retention and security

Personal information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including responding to inquiries and maintaining legitimate business records. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete personal information you submitted through the website, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask questions about how your information is handled by contacting us at onerepcloserct@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

This website and its services are not directed to children under 13, and One Rep Closer LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the inquiry form.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

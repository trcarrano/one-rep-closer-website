Frequently asked questions.

What to expect from personal training and peptide education with One Rep Closer LLC.

Personal training

Training questions.

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

No. You begin exactly where you are. Sessions are adjusted to your current fitness level, experience, and goals.

What if I feel intimidated by the gym?

That is one of the main reasons many clients choose one-on-one training. You receive direct guidance, a clear plan, and support without judgment or competition.

Can workouts be modified for pain or physical limitations?

Exercises can often be modified around mobility limitations, previous injuries, or discomfort. Personal training is not medical treatment, and medical clearance may be requested when appropriate.

Where is personal training offered?

In-person training is offered in the West Haven, Connecticut area. Virtual personal training is also available upon request.

How do the packages work?

Choose the package that fits your schedule and goals. Packages are paid upfront, are non-refundable, and must be used within the time period listed on the Personal Training page.

Do you offer training with a friend?

Yes. The Fitness With a Friend package includes ten shared sessions for $520 and must be used within six months.

Peptide & wellness education

Consultation questions.

What does the $50 consultation include?

It includes one private 40-minute phone or Zoom consultation, personalized educational suggestions based on the information you provide, guidance on sourcing and purchasing, general education on reconstitution, dosing concepts, timing, cycling, storage, and reasonable follow-up questions.

Do I need to complete paperwork first?

Yes. The required acknowledgement and educational-consultation paperwork must be reviewed, signed, and returned before recommendations are provided.

Are peptides included in the consultation fee?

No. Peptides, supplies, and shipping are separate. You are responsible for ordering directly from the source provided.

Will you purchase, mix, or inject peptides for me?

No. One Rep Closer LLC does not purchase products for clients, mix or reconstitute them for clients, administer injections, or inject clients.

Is this medical care?

No. The consultation is educational and does not provide medical care, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, or instructions to start, stop, or change prescription medications.

How can I pay?

The $50 fee may be paid through Venmo at @Tara-Carrano-1 or by credit or debit card upon request.

Does the consultation include unlimited support?

No. Reasonable follow-up help is included for questions about the information discussed, but the service does not include unlimited or on-call access.
